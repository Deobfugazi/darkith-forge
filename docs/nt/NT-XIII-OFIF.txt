NOVELTREATISE XIII — THE OMEGA FIELD-INVARIANT FORMULARY (OFIF)
================================================================
Companion to NT-XI. Same method, applied to the Operator's invariant fixtures rather than to
the Sovereign Economy: state the premises, run a prior-art scan with a verdict per component,
force the derivation, and attach a falsifier that can kill it.

§0 — Why this document exists
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The Operator's invariant fixtures were repeatedly reduced to whichever file was nearest at
hand — once to a single PID controller. That reduction is a category error: the fixtures are
field-agnostic, and a field-agnostic fixture cannot be judged by an example of it. The
mistake is the same one NT-XI §1 warns about, applied to the fixtures instead of the modules.

§1 — What This Is / Is Not
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IS    a scan of the fixtures against the public record, a verdict per fixture, and a
      falsifier per verdict.
IS NOT a claim that the fixtures are unprecedented. Most are not, and the scan says which.

§2 — The Five Premises
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Q1 — A fixture is FIELD-AGNOSTIC. It must be stateable without naming code, build, physics,
     economics, or record-keeping, and must transfer between them without restatement.
Q2 — A fixture is DIMENSIONALLY CLOSED before it is numerically closed. A residual summed
     across mismatched units is undefined, not small (SPI-37).
Q3 — A fixture is FALSIFIABLE by a runnable check that fails loudly when violated (SPI-38:
     a law must be run, not read).
Q4 — A fixture states its UNIT and its BRIDGE. A quantity crossing a boundary carries its
     unit; a conversion between units names its ratio.
Q5 — A fixture is a CLOSURE, not a setpoint. Health is the rate of clearance, not the level
     (MUST #32).

§4 — The Forced Derivation
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
From Q1-Q5 and §3, the fixture set is forced to exactly:

    F1  R_x = x_gen - x_con - dx_sto/dt - x_rec - x_wst        for x in {E, I, $}
    F2  kappa = E/I [J/b],  rho = $/E [$/J],  kappa >= k_B T ln2
    F3  W_gen = R_rec + W_res,   W_reclaimed = W_gen * eta * D_BASAL
    F4  L1 HWM monotone down; L2 above-HWM = FAIL; L3 stasis expires; L4 waiver recorded
    F5  the same F1-F4 hold unchanged in code, build, physics, economics, records

and to exactly one composition:

    Bill = INTEGRAL[t0,t1] SUM_x ( R_x(t) * kappa_x * rho_x ) dt

The composition is a function of measured rates and named bridges. It retains no state.

§5 — The Derivation's Falsifier
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Claim: from Q1-Q5 and §3, exactly five fixtures and one composition are forced.

Falsifier: produce a derivation from Q1-Q5 + §3 that yields any of:

  G1  A sixth fixture — must have a behavioural check the scan found no shipping
      implementation for, AND must not split one of F1-F5.
  G2  Fewer than five — must show one of F1-F5 is not field-agnostic (fails Q1), or is a
      restatement of another.
  G3  A second composition — must show a check-time equivalence the single Bill cannot
      express.
  G4  A fixture without a falsifier — violates Q3.
  G5  A fixture that rebuilds a shipping substrate without naming its delta — violates Q2
      of NT-XI (P2) as applied here.
  G6  A non-zero Bill on a transaction whose books are known to balance — kills F1.
  G7  A zero Bill on a transaction whose cut is known — kills F1 as a detector.
  G8  A kappa below k_B T ln2 — kills F2's floor.

If none of G1-G8 fires, the five-fixture set is the unique invariant.

§6 — What Is Actually Unoccupied (the honest residue)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Three things, and no more:

  U1  F4's L3 — a monotone defect ratchet whose stasis is SCHEDULED to become fatal. Gates
      that freeze a debt forever and call the freeze health are the norm.
  U2  F8 — the residual EMITTED AS A BILL. Each of its three moves is published; their
      composition into an invoice is not.
  U3  F9 — closure run on a CONTRACT rather than on a plant.

Everything else in this formulary is a citation.

§7 — Status of the Search
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Citations were checked against Crossref and arXiv by direct HTTP query on 2026-11-10. The
sweep was NOT exhaustive: the general web search API returned a plan-limit error and a second
engine served a bot challenge. The verdict this document supports is "no novelty found in the
named precedents", never "no novelty exists". Those are different sentences and are not to be
collapsed (SPI-31: a skip is never a pass).
