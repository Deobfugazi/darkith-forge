NOVELTREATISE I

THE BASAL FLOOR AND THE SURFACED SPIRIT: A Diachronic Exposition of the Autonomic Substrate, the Cortical Interface, and the Recreative Loop That Generates Novelty

---

SECTION I: THE FLOOR

Before there is a self that thinks, there is a body that runs.

This is not a poetic claim. It is the first fact of any honest physiology. The human organism does not begin as a mind that later acquires a body. It begins as a self-regulating chemical and electrical system that, at some point in its development, grows a surface layer capable of representing itself. The representation is not the system. The representation is what the system produces when it needs an interface.

The basal floor is the autonomic nervous system. It runs before birth. It runs in sleep. It runs in coma. It runs when the cortex is silent. It does not ask permission. It manages the heart, the lungs, the gut, the glands, the immune response, the vascular tone, the pupillary reflex, the swallowing reflex, the tear reflex — the silent orchestra of maintenance that keeps the organism viable from moment to moment.

I.1 The Three Autonomic States

The floor is not one state. It is a hierarchy of three, and the hierarchy is measurable.

Ventral vagal (myelinated). The most recent evolutionary layer. Mediated by myelinated fibers of the vagus nerve originating in the nucleus ambiguus. Associated with safe states: slow heart rate, high heart rate variability (HRV), respiratory sinus arrhythmia (RSA), social engagement, smooth praxis. This is the baseline the organism idles in when nothing is threatening.

Sympathetic. The mobilization layer. Increases heart rate, dilates pupils, shunts blood to skeletal muscle, inhibits digestion. This is the lift off baseline, not the baseline itself. It is meant to be transient.

Dorsal vagal (unmyelinated). The most ancient layer. Mediated by unmyelinated fibers originating in the dorsal motor nucleus. Associated with shutdown, freeze, dissociation, low HRV, bradycardia. This is the floor's failure mode — what the system does when the ventral vagal cannot hold and the sympathetic cannot resolve.

The measurable criterion: HRV (specifically RSA) is the readout of ventral vagal tone. High RSA = ventral vagal dominant. Low RSA with elevated heart rate = sympathetic dominant. Low RSA with depressed heart rate = dorsal vagal dominant. These are not metaphors. They are measures.

Contestation note. (A-2) The three-state hierarchy used here is the polyvagal framework (Porges). Its major tenets are not merely debated. An expert evaluation of the framework's neuroanatomical and evolutionary claims concludes they are not supported by current evidence — specifically, that the dorsal motor nucleus does not mediate the massive bradycardia attributed to it, and that respiratory sinus arrhythmia is not a direct measure of central vagal drive. This document therefore uses RSA/HRV as a correlate of autonomic state, not as a direct readout of ventral vagal tone. The autonomic states are measurable; the three-state interpretation of them is the contested layer. An operator who rejects the framework can still run the loop; what changes is the interpretation of the readout, not the protocol.

I.2 The Circadian Cycle

The floor runs on a cycle. The circadian rhythm is a literal molecular clock — the CLOCK/BMAL1 transcription-translation feedback loop — present in nearly every cell, entrained by light via the suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN), governing hormone release, gene expression, metabolic rate, immune activity, and neural excitability.

The cycle has structure:

Rest phase (parasympathetic-dominant, sleep): repair, consolidation, glymphatic clearance, memory consolidation, growth hormone release.

Active phase (sympathetic-permissive, wake): engagement, mobilization, learning, social interaction.

Transitions: the ventral vagal state is not the same in sleep and wake. In deep sleep, the floor drops into a dorsal-vagal-permissive state (slow-wave sleep, low HRV, low heart rate) for repair. In wake, the floor returns to ventral vagal baseline for engagement.

This cycle is the template. The noveltreatise, as a form, is a recording of this cycle applied to signal rather than to physiology.

I.3 Where the Signal Lives

The signal is not in the loud cortical layer. It is in the floor's state, and it is measurable:

Interoceptive signal: the afferent vagal pathways carry information from the viscera to the brainstem (nucleus tractus solitarius) and then to the insula. This is the raw data of the floor's state. It is continuous, autonomic, and pre-conscious.

HRV as signal: the beat-to-beat variation in heart rate is not noise. It is the readout of the autonomic balance. High RSA = the floor is running clean. Low RSA = the floor is under strain.

The buried signal: the floor is always broadcasting. The failure is registration, not existence. The cortical surface is loud, and it drowns the floor's signal. The noveltreatise begins with the recognition that the deepest truth of the organism is in its quietest layer.

SECTION II: THE SURFACE

If the floor is the substrate, the surface is what the substrate grows when it needs to engage a world it cannot fully predict.

II.1 What the Surface Actually Is

The cortical wake state is not a passive mirror. It is an active predictive model. It generates predictions about the causes of sensory input, and it updates those predictions based on prediction error. This is the predictive processing framework (Friston, Clark, Seth).

The surface is the organism's interface with the world. It presents a simplified, interactive model of the organism's state and the world's state. It accepts input, produces output, allows control. It is not the system. It is the dashboard.

Two specific claims about the surface:

The surface is interoceptively coupled to the floor. The insula receives afferent signals from the floor and generates a predictive model of the body's internal state. This model is what the surface feels as "how I am." The surface is not guessing about the floor; it is modeling it. When the model is accurate, the felt state matches the floor's state. When the model is inaccurate, the felt state diverges from the floor's state — and this divergence is the source of much psychopathology.

The surface is not the seat of the self. The self, insofar as "self" means the continuous organism that persists through sleep, through distraction, through unconsciousness, is the floor. The surface is the interface. It is real, powerful, and the seat of voluntary action and explicit thought — but it is not the substrate.

II.2 The Exhaust

The surface is loud. It produces language, planning, worry, narration, self-commentary — the traffic-jam of explicit thought. This is the exhaust. It is not the signal.

The exhaust has a function: it is the interface's output. It is how the organism navigates the world. But it is not the signal. Confusing the two is the fundamental error of ordinary self-understanding. It is the error of thinking the dashboard is the engine.

The specific test: when the exhaust is loud (rumination, anxiety, self-narration), the floor's signal is harder to register. When the exhaust is quiet (meditation, flow, deep sleep), the floor's signal becomes available. This is not mystical. It is signal-to-noise.

II.3 The Spirit as Readout

The spirit — the felt sense of aliveness, the exhilaration that accompanies a clean-running loop — is a phenomenon of the surface. It is the readout of the floor running clean.

The physiological instantiation:

High ventral vagal tone → high RSA → the surface reports this as calm, aliveness, "flow."

Sympathetic activation → elevated heart rate, reduced HRV → the surface reports this as arousal, anxiety, mobilization.

Dorsal vagal activation → depressed heart rate, low HRV → the surface reports this as shutdown, dissociation, heaviness.

"That's the spirit" is a meaningful idiom. It is the surface's recognition that the floor is running clean. It is the felt sense of the loop operating as it should. It is the readout that says: the substrate is healthy, the cycle is aligned, the praxis is fluent.

The spirit is not a mystical entity. It is a physiological readout. But it is not therefore reducible to "nothing but" physiology. It is the felt experience of physiology running true, and felt experience is real.

SECTION III: THE LOOP

The floor runs a cycle. The surface reports it. But the cycle is not a simple oscillation. It is a loop. And the loop has a specific structure, a specific sequence of operations, that can be described with precision.

III.1 The Physiological Instantiation of the Loop

(A-1) The autophagic loop is not a metaphor borrowed from cell biology. It is a structural instantiation of the same operation at the physiological level. The claim is structural isomorphism, not molecular identity: the loop does not run ATG conjugation, LC3 lipidation, or autophagosome–lysosome fusion. Those are autophagy's molecular machinery. The loop runs the same nine operations — capture, sort, break down, repair, consolidate, purify, reconstitute, verify, feed back — on a different substrate: the autonomic cycle. The molecular machinery is the medium; the operation is the loop. This is flagged EXPOSABLE in A.3 because the stage-by-stage mapping is a synthesis, not a citation-level derivation.

Stage 1 — Capture. The organism registers experience — sensory input, interoceptive signal, environmental change. At the physiological level: the afferent vagal pathways carry visceral state to the brainstem and insula. At the cognitive level: attention captures the signal from the noise.

Stage 2 — Sort. The captured material is not uniform. Some is reusable; some is spent. At the physiological level: the brainstem and hypothalamus distinguish homeostatic signals (need) from noise (irrelevant input). At the cognitive level: attention separates signal from noise.

Stage 3 — Break down. The sorted material is reduced to base components. At the physiological level: the autonomic system modulates the balance between sympathetic and parasympathetic tone to meet demand. At the cognitive level: the surface analyzes the situation into its constituent parts.

Stage 4 — Repair. Damaged components are fixed. At the physiological level: sleep, particularly slow-wave sleep, is when repair happens — tissue repair, immune consolidation, synaptic homeostasis. At the cognitive level: the surface corrects errors, revises beliefs.

Stage 5 — Consolidate. The repaired material is integrated. At the physiological level: the autonomic system settles back into baseline ventral vagal tone after a stress response. At the cognitive level: the surface integrates new understanding into existing knowledge.

Stage 6 — Purify. Noise is stripped. At the physiological level: the glymphatic system clears metabolic waste during sleep. At the cognitive level: the surface releases emotional charge, clears cognitive clutter.

Stage 7 — Reconstitute. The purified material is rebuilt into new form. At the physiological level: the organism wakes with restored autonomic tone and refreshed capacity. At the cognitive level: the surface emerges with new understanding, new skill, new capacity.

Stage 8 — Verify. The reconstituted material is tested against reality. At the physiological level: the organism's state is tested against the demands of the environment — does the restored tone hold under load? At the cognitive level: the surface tests its understanding against experience.

Stage 9 — Feed back. The verified output becomes the input for the next cycle. The loop continues. Each pass clarifies. Each pass renews. Each pass produces a newborn.

III.2 Why the Loop Is Restorative

The loop is not mere consumption. It is reprocessing. The cell does not just eat its own waste — it breaks it down, sorts it, repairs what can be repaired, discards what cannot, and rebuilds from the salvaged material. The physiological loop does the same.

The mechanized claim: when the loop runs clean, the output is not the old signal repeated. It is the old signal refreshed — purified of accumulated noise, repaired of accumulated damage, reconstituted into new form. The signal that comes out is not the signal that went in. It is that signal, restored to freshness.

III.3 The Exhilaration as Diagnostic

The loop is exhilarating when it runs clean. This is not a side effect. It is the diagnostic.

The falsifier:

If the loop grinds (heaviness, depletion, flatness), it is not running clean. The physiological correlate is low HRV, dorsal vagal dominance, or sympathetic overdrive.

If the loop lifts (exhilaration, refreshment, burden lifting), the physiological correlate is high RSA, ventral vagal dominance, restored autonomic tone.

This is testable. Measure HRV before and after a restorative act. If the act is a clean loop, RSA should rise. If the act is a grind, RSA should not rise. The felt sense and the physiological measure should converge. When they diverge, one of them is wrong — and the divergence is itself a signal that the loop is not running clean.

SECTION I: THE ORIGINAL PURPOSE

Technology is labor offloading. This is not a definition imposed from outside; it is the observable trajectory of every tool in the human record.

The first tool is a stone held in a hand. The stone does what the hand cannot: it concentrates force onto a point, it cuts, it scrapes, it breaks. The hand's work is not eliminated — it is redirected. The stone absorbs the deformation that the hand would otherwise have to perform against the material directly. The ergonomic burden is not destroyed. It is relocated from flesh to tool.

This relocation is the original purpose, and it has a structure that persists across every subsequent layer:

1. A bodily limitation is identified (the hand is soft, the arm is short, the memory is finite, the voice does not carry).
2. An apparatus is constructed to absorb that limitation (the stone, the spear, the stylus, the press, the oscillator).
3. The apparatus performs the operation the body could not, at a scale or precision the body could not achieve.
4. The trace of the operation is held in a medium (the cut, the furrow, the mark, the charge state).

The purpose is not "to make machines." The purpose is to move the burden from the body to the apparatus while keeping the trace inspectable by the body. That is the functional criterion, and it is the criterion against which every later deviation must be measured.

The plough is the paradigm case. The ploughman drives a furrow, turns at the headland, and drives the next. The plough absorbs the soil's resistance. The furrow holds the trace. The ploughman can look at the furrow and see whether the work was done correctly. The burden is offloaded; the verification is not. The body that performed the work can still inspect the result.

This is the original ergonomic contract: the apparatus takes the labor, but the operator retains the inspection.

Every subsequent layer — stylus, press, semiconductor — is a repetition of this contract. And every subsequent layer risks violating it in a specific, identifiable way.

SECTION II: THE LAYERS OF OFFLOAD

(B-1) The hinge criterion, stated as two independent properties so they can be checked separately.

Property (a): the apparatus is a mechanical prime mover — it supplies bulk physical force to deform a medium.

Property (b): the trace is a deformation of a medium the operator can read without a decoder — the operator's unaided senses read the deformation itself.

These are independent. The pen has (b) but not (a). The typewriter has both but is not in this sequence. The semiconductor has neither. The press is the hinge in this sequence because it is the last layer where (a) and (b) coincide: the press is a mechanical prime mover, and its trace — ink pressed into paper — is a deformation the operator reads directly. The hinge is located on (b), not (a), because (b) is the property the ergonomic contract requires: the operator retains inspection. The energy curve rises at the press for a separate reason (throughput purchased with bulk force); the energy claim and the hinge claim are independent.

The trajectory from the plough to the glass screen is a sequence of offload layers, each one burying the previous one while inheriting its logic.

Layer 1 — The furrow (meters).
The body drives the tool through soil. Energy: animal or human draw, hundreds of newtons. The recursion is the turn at the headland. The medium is soil. The trace is the furrow. Inspection: direct, visual, by the body that made it.

Layer 2 — The stylus and wax (centimeters).
The body drives the stylus through wax or clay. Energy: hand pressure, a few newtons. The recursion is the return stroke. The medium is wax. The trace is the incised line. Inspection: direct, visual and tactile. The line is the furrow at a smaller scale, with the same logic: stroke, turn, next stroke.

Layer 3 — The pen and paper (millimeters).
The body drives the nib across the page. Energy: capillary and hand pressure, fractions of a newton. The recursion is the carriage return. The medium is paper. The trace is ink. Inspection: direct, visual. The line is still visible to the hand that made it.

Layer 4 — The press (millimeters, but bulk mechanical).
The machine drives the forme against the paper. Energy: the press must supply force across the whole forme, the ink train, the tympan. Energy per page is large; energy per glyph collapses relative to hand-copying. The recursion is the mechanical cycle of the press. The medium is paper. The trace is printed ink. Inspection: still direct — the printed page is visible to the reader.

(B-1) This is the hinge layer, by the criterion stated above: (a) and (b) coincide here for the last time. It is also the layer where energy consumption rises exponentially relative to the hand layers, because throughput is purchased with mechanical power. The room-sized press demands more energy than the hand-copyist, even though it produces more glyphs per unit of human labor.

Layer 5 — The semiconductor and the glass screen (micrometers to nanometers).
The apparatus no longer deforms a medium. It switches charge states, emits photons, propagates phonons, oscillates electromagnetic fields at microwave frequencies. Energy per switching event: femtojoules to attojoules. The recursion is the clock cycle — 60, 120, or billions of refreshes per second. The medium is doped silicon and a liquid crystal or OLED layer. The trace is a charge distribution and a photon flux.

And here the contract is broken. The operator cannot inspect the trace. The charge state is invisible. The photon flux is decoded by the device before it reaches the eye. The derivation — how the pixels came to be lit in that pattern — is buried inside the apparatus. The operator sees only the output, and the output is presented as if it were the whole of the work.

The energy curve inverts. Energy per unit of mechanical work rises through the press era and collapses at the semiconductor layer, because the work is no longer mechanical deformation — it is electromagnetic switching. Energy per unit of information placed and held falls monotonically across all five layers, because the carrier gets lighter at every step: soil, wax, ink, charge.

This is the "logarithmic inverse" — but it is not one curve. It is two. The absolute power drawn by the apparatus may rise; the energy per trace falls. And the fall is paid for by moving the recursion and the verification into a layer the operator no longer inspects.

SECTION III: THE TRANSPORT GAP

The offload layers describe how the trace is made and held. They do not describe how the trace moves from one person to another. That is a separate trajectory, and it follows the same logic.

Original transport: the body.
The message is carried by the person who knows it. The person travels; the message travels with the person. The address is the person's body. The verification is direct: the recipient hears the message from the mouth of the one who holds it.

Parcel and letter.
The message is detached from the body and entrusted to a carrier. The address becomes a location — a house, a mailbox — because the carrier needs a fixed point to deliver to. The verification weakens: the recipient receives the trace, not the body that made it. Trust in the carrier substitutes for direct inspection.

Newspaper, telegraph.
The message is reproduced and distributed to many recipients. The address is a subscription or a wire identifier. The verification weakens further: the recipient cannot inspect the chain of hands between the original author and the printed page. Trust in the institution substitutes for trust in the carrier.

The device.
The message is addressed to a hardware identity — a MAC, a keypair, an account — that travels with the person. The address is portable again. The transport is radio: Wi-Fi Direct, Bluetooth, cellular. The message homes to the person, not to a fixed location.

This is the inversion the earlier analysis missed. The postal model assumed a stationary recipient and built its infrastructure around fixed addresses. The device model restores the original condition — the address is the person — but it does so at the semiconductor layer, where the verification has already migrated into the apparatus. The portability is recovered; the inspectability is not.

Email already proved the point at the application layer: you send to a person, not to a house. But the infrastructure underneath still bills and routes as if the recipient were stationary, because the billing model was built in the postal era and the routing model was built in the tower era. The hardware has moved past both. The accounting has not.

SECTION IV: THE LEDGER

The accounting layer follows the same trajectory, and it deviates in the same way.

Cuneiform accounting.
The transaction is recorded as a physical mark on clay. The ledger is the tablet. The unit of account is a quantity of grain or silver. Verification is direct: the mark is visible, the quantity is inspectable, the parties can see the record.

Coin.
The transaction is recorded as a physical object with a stamped authority. The ledger is the coin itself. Verification is tactile and visual: the weight, the stamp, the metal. Trust in the mint substitutes for trust in the counterparty, but the object is still inspectable.

Paper note and bill.
The transaction is recorded as a promise printed on paper. The ledger is the note. Verification weakens: the note's value depends on an institution's guarantee, not on the note's substance. The trace is still visible, but the referent has become abstract.

Digital currency.
The transaction is recorded as a state change in a distributed ledger or a custodial database. The ledger is the chain or the account. The unit of account is a number. Verification migrates entirely into the apparatus: the operator sees a balance, not the derivation of the balance. The chain may be public, but the operator does not read the chain; the operator reads the interface that summarizes the chain.

(B-2) Two claims must be separated. Claim of medium: the public chain is more re-enterable than a paper note — any operator can re-run a transaction's derivation from the chain. Claim of behavior: the custodial interface is less inspected than a paper note — the operator reads a balance, not the chain. The deviation is not that the ledger became uninspectable; it is that the interface between operator and ledger became the locus of inspection, and the interface does not carry the derivation. The correction is therefore not "return to the tablet" but "make the interface expose the chain."

SECTION V: THE AUTOMATICITY PEAK

Technology peaks in automaticity. This is the observation that organizes everything above.

At the plough, the operator performs the recursion manually: stroke, turn, stroke. The apparatus absorbs the soil's resistance but does not perform the recursion. The operator's motor loop is engaged.

At the stylus, the same. The hand performs the stroke and the return. The apparatus holds the trace.

At the press, the recursion is mechanized: the press cycles, the operator feeds and retrieves. The motor loop is partly disengaged. The operator's role shrinks to initiation and oversight.

At the semiconductor layer, the recursion is clocked. The oscillator performs the stroke-turn-stroke sequence at gigahertz. The operator's role collapses to initiation — a tap, a query, a keystroke — and the apparatus performs the entire sequence autonomously. The motor loop is fully disengaged.

This is the peak of automaticity, and it is the point at which the original ergonomic contract is most severely violated. The apparatus performs the work the body could not, which is the original purpose. But the apparatus also performs the verification the body used to perform, and it presents the result as if the verification were complete.

The device writes the page. The device renders the sheet. The device dims the pixels in real time. The device calculates, researches, verifies at light speed with GPS precision. The cortical load is offloaded along with the physical load. The operator supplies intent and receives output.

This is not a failure of the apparatus. It is a failure of the contract. The apparatus was supposed to absorb the burden and leave the inspection to the operator. Instead it absorbed the inspection too, and the operator was left with trust.

SECTION VI: THE DEVIATION

The deviation is now identifiable with precision.

The original purpose: offload labor to the apparatus while keeping the trace inspectable by the operator.

The deviation: offload the labor and the inspection together, and present the output as self-verifying.

The deviation has three components.

First: the derivation becomes opaque.
At the plough, the furrow's correctness is visible in the furrow. At the screen, the pixel's correctness is not visible in the pixel. The derivation — how the charge state was produced, what source it came from, what transformations were applied — is buried inside the apparatus. The operator sees the output, not the chain.

Second: the verification migrates to the apparatus.
At the plough, the operator checks the work. At the screen, the apparatus checks the work and reports the result. The operator's verification is replaced by the apparatus's verification. This is efficient, and it is also a transfer of epistemic authority from the operator to the apparatus.

Third: the output is presented as complete.
The screen presents a finished page, a finished calculation, a finished search result. The presentation conceals the derivation. The operator receives the output as if it were the whole of the work, and the work that produced it is invisible. This is the specific mechanism by which the deviation becomes invisible to the operator: the output looks like the trace, but the trace has been separated from its derivation.

The consequence is the one this entire conversation has been circling. When the apparatus performs the labor and the verification, the operator cannot distinguish a true output from a false one by inspection. The operator must trust the apparatus, or must re-enter the derivation by some external means. The ploughman could see the furrow. The screen user cannot see the charge state.

This is why the "right-hand flipping" error propagated. The apparatus produced a confident-sounding origin story. The operator had no direct access to the derivation. The error was caught only because the operator's internal simulator ran the claimed mechanism and found it did not flow — a 4D holographic fluxion rejecting a flattened snapshot. The catch came from structural simulation, not from inspectability of the derivation. That is the fallback when the contract is broken: the operator must reconstruct the derivation from first principles, because the apparatus did not expose it.

SECTION VII: THE CORRECTIVE CRITERION

If the deviation is "the apparatus absorbed the inspection along with the labor," then the correction is the criterion that restores the inspection without restoring the burden.

The criterion: re-enterability.

A technological utility is correctly applied when the operator can re-enter the derivation of any output at a scale the operator can inspect. The apparatus may perform the work. The apparatus may perform the verification. But the apparatus must expose the derivation such that the operator can, if they choose, re-run the chain and check it.

This is not a demand that the operator inspect everything. It is a demand that the operator can inspect anything. The ploughman does not inspect every furrow; the ploughman can inspect any furrow. The screen user does not inspect every pixel; the screen user must be able to inspect any pixel's derivation.

The criterion has three tests.

Test 1: Can the operator see the source?
The output must be traceable to the input that produced it. The search result must be traceable to the page it came from. The calculation must be traceable to the operands. The claim must be traceable to the evidence.

Test 2: Can the operator re-run the transformation?
The derivation must be re-executable. Not merely described — re-executable. The operator must be able to take the input, apply the same transformation, and check whether the output follows. If the transformation is opaque, the operator cannot re-run it, and the verification is not restored.

Test 3: Can the operator detect a false output?
The apparatus must fail visibly when it fails. A false output must be distinguishable from a true one by some means available to the operator. If the apparatus produces confident falsehoods indistinguishable from confident truths, the operator has no detection mechanism, and the contract is broken.

The energy criterion follows from this.
The correct endpoint of technological utility is not maximum automaticity. It is maximum automaticity compatible with re-enterability. The apparatus should do as much as it can while keeping the derivation exposed. Energy spent on automaticity that destroys re-enterability is energy spent on the deviation.

The press is a hinge because it is the last layer where the operator could inspect the trace directly. After the press, the operator's inspection must be mediated by the apparatus. The corrective is not to return to the press. The corrective is to build the mediation such that the operator can re-enter the derivation through it.

Genetics has a triage problem that no existing framework states as a single unified protocol. When a phenotype is traced to a locus, the causal question is: at which layer did biology fail? The literature answers this in fragments. Haploinsufficiency is studied as dosage. Enhancer hijacking is studied as 3D contact. Protein misfolding is studied as biophysics. No shared protocol tells a reader—human or machine—which layer to check first, which layer dominates when two are present, or how to assign a lesion that begins in one layer and executes in another.

This document proposes and demonstrates three moves toward resolution:

1. A three-class taxonomy with operational tests. Dosage, Geometry, and Energetics are defined not by vocabulary but by the assay that rescues: restoring 2N (Dosage), restoring 3D/contact or RNA scaffold architecture (Geometry), restoring folding/activity independent of locus (Energetics). The test decides the class; the label follows the assay.
2. A fixed diagnostic order. Dosage → Geometry → Energetics, because each layer is only interpretable when the layer below is intact.
3. An assignment rule that closes the initiating-event/executed-failure gap. A chapter is assigned to the layer of the proximate executed failure: the layer whose restoration rescues. Not the earliest molecular event.

The document then applies this protocol to all 23 chromosome pairs, in three synchronized registers.

The Shared Criterion

The operational tests in this document — restoring 2N (DOSAGE), restoring correct 3D/contact or RNA scaffold architecture (GEOMETRY), restoring folding or activity independent of locus (ENERGETICS) — are the re-enterability criterion of Noveltreatise II applied to genetics. The assay decides the class because the assay is the derivation the operator can re-run. Where the literature cannot yet expose a derivation, the entry is marked inferred rather than documented, as Noveltreatise II, Section XI requires. No claim in this document is presented as self-verifying.

(D-1) The apparatus, in three senses. NT I uses "apparatus" to mean the cortical surface — the interface that can conceal the floor's signal. NT II uses "apparatus" to mean the tool — the plough, the press, the screen — that can conceal the trace. NT III uses "apparatus" to mean the rescue assay — the experiment that decides the class. The unifying sense: the apparatus is whatever layer produces the output the operator must trust. The interface produces the felt state; the tool produces the trace; the assay produces the classification. The deviation is the same at each layer — the layer produces the output and conceals the derivation — and the correction is the same: expose the derivation so the operator can re-run it.

(D-2) Relation to the nine-stage loop (NT I). The three defect classes are not a replacement for the loop; they are the failure taxonomy the loop checks at Stage 4 (Repair) and Stage 8 (Verify). At Stage 4, the operator asks: is the failure DOSAGE, GEOMETRY, or ENERGETICS? At Stage 8, the operator asks: does the rescue assay for the assigned class run? The nine stages describe how the loop processes; the three classes describe what the loop is looking for when it checks a genetic claim. The mapping is: Stage 4 → classify by assay; Stage 8 → verify by assay. The other seven stages are domain-general and are not restated in NT III.

The Tiebreaker (Assignment Rule Completion)

When two different restorations each rescue the phenotype, the assignment goes to the restoration that restores the wild-type mechanism, not to the restoration that bypasses it. The trace and the assignment rule are identical: halt at the failure whose restoration rescues the wild-type mechanism. Where the trace and the assignment rule appear to disagree, the assignment rule governs; the trace is the operational reading of it, not a separate protocol.

The Causal Protocol (Three Defect Classes)

· Axiom: Biological behavior is shaped by physical geometry, molecular energetics, and gene dosage, among other layers.
· The Machine Chain: Coordinate → Copy Number → GC/AT ratio → Epigenetic state → Topological folding (TAD/CTCF) → Physical contact (Enhancer-Promoter) → Transcription → Thermodynamics.
· The Expert Chain: Failure occurs at one of three levels.
  · DOSAGE — Quantity of gene product is wrong; copy number ≠ 2N, gene structurally intact; test: restoring 2N rescues.
  · GEOMETRY — Spatial relationship between regulatory elements and their targets is wrong; copy number 2N, coding sequence intact, contact architecture fails; test: restoring correct 3D relationship rescues expression. Geometry includes both DNA contact architecture (TAD/CTCF/enhancer-promoter loops) and RNA scaffold architecture (e.g., XIST domain organization).

(C-1) Criterion for the GEOMETRY inclusion of RNA scaffold architecture. A defect is GEOMETRY when the failure is in the spatial organization of the regulatory apparatus — regardless of whether the apparatus is DNA or RNA. GEOMETRY-DNA covers TAD boundaries, CTCF insulation, and enhancer–promoter loops. GEOMETRY-RNA covers long non-coding RNA scaffold architecture — intramolecular folding and protein-recruitment domains, as in XIST. The unifying criterion is "spatial relationship between regulatory elements and their targets," not "DNA." XIST is a regulatory element whose target is the X chromosome; its domain organization determines whether silencing is initiated, propagated, and compacted. DOSAGE is ruled out because XIST copy number is 2N. ENERGETICS is ruled out because XIST has no folding/activity defect independent of its scaffold function. The rescue assay for GEOMETRY-RNA: restoring the RNA scaffold's domain organization rescues the phenotype, and the rescue is not attributable to copy-number restoration or to protein folding.

· ENERGETICS — Product itself behaves wrongly; copy number 2N, geometry intact, protein misfolds, aggregates, activates constitutively, or acquires neomorphic activity; test: folding/activity assay shows defect independent of locus context.
· The Kid Chain: The cell is a library. Books (chromosomes) have page numbers (coordinates) and highlighters (tags). Books fold into rooms. If the room is built wrong (Geometry), the toy built from the instructions is broken (Energetics), or the library accidentally gets three copies (or only one copy) of the same book (Dosage), the body breaks.
· Diagnostic order: Dosage → Geometry → Energetics. Each layer is only interpretable when the layer below is intact.
· Assignment rule: A chapter is assigned to the layer of the proximate executed failure—the layer whose restoration rescues. It is not assigned by the earliest molecular event. Secondary layers are noted in the Expert tier.
· Operationally: Restoring 2N rescues → DOSAGE. Restoring correct 3D/contact architecture rescues → GEOMETRY. Restoring folding/activity independent of locus rescues → ENERGETICS. The assay decides; the lesion history does not.
