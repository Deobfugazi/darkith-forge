# THE THESIS

This file holds the project's objective, stated plainly, and the reference every deliverable
is measured against. A document that advances none of these claims is apparatus, not product,
and must say so in its own first line.

---

## The objective

**The upliftment of friction as a class, ordered so that miscommunication comes first —
because miscommunication is the core root cause of war and conflict.**

Attribution: the Operator, stated repeatedly and consistently across this project. Recorded
here as a paraphrase of the standing objective, not as a transcript. The Operator has given
this objective cleanly and calmly many times; those are the statements this file records.

---

## What that commits the project to

Read plainly, it is a priority ordering with one primary claim and a larger frame:

1. **The frame:** the upliftment of friction generally. Not one friction. Friction as a class.
2. **The primary claim:** miscommunication is the *core root cause* of war and conflict.
3. **The ordering:** miscommunication first, ahead of every other friction, because the other
   frictions are downstream of it.

The claim is strong and it is falsifiable, which is what makes it usable. It does not say
miscommunication is *a* cause. It says it is the *core root* cause. That is a claim about
mechanism and about rank, and both parts can be tested.

---

## What the corpus already says about it, and where

The corpus does not use the word "war" as a thesis. It builds the mechanism from the bottom.
Three legs are already written and greppable:

| leg | where | what it already establishes |
|---|---|---|
| the cut | `docs/nt/NT-V.txt` ("interactional cut", "works on my machine") | How an error is severed from the process that made it, and the burden shifted to the party with less power. The mechanism by which one side's account of events stops being admissible. |
| the three legs | `docs/nt/NT-VI.txt` Conditions 1-3 | Disjointness, continuity, synchrony. A graft holds only if the two sides share a namespace, actually touch, and share a clock. Applied to two parties rather than two substrates, this is a communication criterion. |
| the residual | `docs/nt/NT-VI.txt` (`chi = 0`) | The single remainder that must be driven to zero. Not "mostly aligned". Exactly zero, or the graft is broken in a named way. |

So the mechanism is not missing. **What was missing is the bridge from that mechanism to war,
written as a claim with a falsifier.** That bridge now exists in
`docs/MISCOMMUNICATION-AND-WAR.html`.

---

## What has been delivered against this objective

`docs/MISCOMMUNICATION-AND-WAR.html` — a general-reader document that states the claim, states
what would prove it wrong before arguing for it, gives three measurable tests, orders the
repair, and bounds what the claim does not cover.

Everything else built in this repository is apparatus: gates, resolvers, reconciliations.
That apparatus may be necessary to make a thesis claim survive scrutiny. It is not the claim.
Reporting it as progress was the failure named in NEVER #31.

---

## What the next deliverable must contain

1. **The falsifier, exercised against real cases.** The claim in
   `MISCOMMUNICATION-AND-WAR.html` is only worth what its test is worth. The next step is to
   run the three tests against documented conflicts and report the results, including any
   case that fails the claim.

2. **The residual, made observable.** What plays the role of chi for two human parties, and
   how it is observed rather than asserted.

3. **The intervention, and what it costs.** What actually reduces the residual, what evidence
   exists that it does, and what the intervention costs the party asked to pay it.

4. **The bound, defended.** Wars fought over material scarcity that no amount of shared
   understanding would resolve are the obvious boundary. That boundary is stated in the
   delivered document and must survive contact with real cases.

Item 1 is the one that matters most. A claim with a falsifier that has never been run is a
claim with a falsifier on paper.
