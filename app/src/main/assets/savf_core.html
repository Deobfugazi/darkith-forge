Supported claim grammar & engine notes

Numeric & calculus

  • d/dx of EXPR at N is M — symbolic derivative of an expression in x, evaluated at N.
  • The integral of EXPR from A to B is M — adaptive Simpson quadrature on [A,B].
  • The exponential of N is M / The logarithm base B of N is M.
  • The sine|cosine|tangent of N is M.
  • The determinant of [[A, B], [C, D]] is M — a 2×2 determinant.
  • EXPR equals EXPR — arithmetic identity (no x).

Facts, units, axes

  • The PROPERTY of ENTITY is VALUE — looked up in the payload as entity:property.
  • A meters plus B meters equals C meters — dimensional check + scale-aware arithmetic.
  • The row|column|depth axis traverses DIRECTION on the math|raster|sbaf0 chart — handedness sign τ.

Reasoning

  • assume ID — declares an axiom.
  • ID derives from ID [and ID ...] — a derivation; all parents must be declared or verified.

Expression language

  • Operators + - * / ^, parentheses, implicit multiplication (2x), unary minus.
  • Functions sin cos tan exp log sqrt abs; constants pi, e, and any payload constants.
  • Words are normalised: plus times minus divided by squared cubed to the N, and number words.

Verdict rule

  • LIED when a claim in a self-contained gate was evaluated and found false (arithmetic, calculus, trigonometry, derivation).
  • BLOCKED when a claim could not be established — malformed, unsupported, missing from the payload, or resting on a convention not attested.
  • REVIEW when no claims were submitted.
  • PASS only when every claim was declared or verified.
  • The gate strip shows each phi gate as pass, marginal, fail, or n/a when no claim touched it.
