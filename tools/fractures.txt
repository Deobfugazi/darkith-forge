# fractures.txt — the Fracture Scout manifest (the ONE place to configure the hunt).
#
# A "Fracture" (NT-V §XIX.15) is a real thing in the world, anchored to a real problem.
# The scout seeks current world fractures and unresolved bottlenecks that the omega
# formulation can work on. It is NOT a naive keyword search: a query here is a boolean
# expression — OR between alternatives, AND within one, "quoted phrases" kept whole — so
# it matches a gap ("no known fix", "open problem"), not a topic word in isolation.
#
# Format, one line per source query:
#   <source>   <query expression>   [weight]
#
#   source   arxiv | nvd | hn | github-issues | ietf | rss
#   query    boolean expression: term AND "quoted phrase" OR alternative ...
#   weight   optional 1..5 importance multiplier (default 1)
#
# Blank lines and # comments are ignored. Whitespace-separated, so it survives a phone,
# a diff, and any editor. This file, not a vendor's config, is the source of truth:
# tools/fracture_scout.py reads it, fetches each source over HTTPS, and ranks what it
# finds by novelty and unresolved-ness. It never executes anything it downloads.

# ── research front: the unsolved, stated as unsolved ───────────────────────────────
arxiv    "open problem" OR "unsolved" OR "remains an open challenge"    3
arxiv    "no known" AND ("algorithm" OR "construction") OR "hardness"   2

# ── security fractures: a named defect with a real blast radius ────────────────────
nvd      severity:CRITICAL                                               3
nvd      severity:HIGH                                                   2

# ── practitioner bottlenecks: where the field says it is stuck ─────────────────────
hn       "no known fix" OR "bottleneck" OR "unsolved" OR "why is this still"  2
hn       "regression" OR "impossible" OR "blocker"                        1

# ── real unresolved problems, admitted by the people who hold them ─────────────────
github-issues  label:"good first issue" is:open                          1
github-issues  "bug" OR "regression" is:open state:open                  1

# ── protocol gaps: standards work that is not done ─────────────────────────────────
ietf     "gap" OR "unresolved" OR "open issue"                           1
